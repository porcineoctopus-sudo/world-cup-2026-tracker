Champion-chance trajectory

Each team's title odds heading into each round. A team gets a line once its chance reaches 1% and keeps it from then on, so you can watch a contender rise and fall. An eliminated team's line stops at the round it reached, its flag greyed out. Teams that have never reached 1% are tallied below.

Most likely finals

Match-ups at 1% or more get a card. The big number is how often that exact final occurs; each side's figure is its chance of winning that game. These are specific pairings, so a team can be a 1%+ title pick without any single 1%+ final.
